How to Play

Goal: Form 4-in-a-row or place all pieces

Pieces: Regular (Owls/Squirrels), Houses, and Koala

Actions: Place a piece adjacent to others, or eject an opponent's piece with yours

Eject rules: House ejects Regular • Koala ejects Regular or House • (Koala can't be ejected)

Your turn: Drag pieces from your supply to the board to play.
