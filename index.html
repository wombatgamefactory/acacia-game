How to play

Goal

Be the first to make a line of four of your pieces — across, down or diagonally — or to get every piece out of your supply and onto the board.

Your pieces

8 × Regular Place on any empty space that touches a piece already on the board (including diagonally). Counts toward a line.
4 × Door Ejects an opponent's regular piece and takes its space. The ejected piece goes back to their supply. Counts toward a line.
1 × Koala Ejects an opponent's door (or regular piece). Never counts toward a line — for you or for them — so it also blocks.

On your turn

Take one action: place a regular piece, or eject with a door or koala. Then it is your opponent's turn.

Winning

  • Four in a row wins immediately — any mix of your regular and door pieces.
  • Koalas break lines and count for nobody.
  • Placing your last piece also wins immediately.

Playing on screen

Tap one of your pieces, then tap a glowing space. On a computer you can also drag a piece straight onto the board.
